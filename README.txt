# Mini Market Stock

A simple phone app for running a mini market. Add your products, track stock and expiry dates, see your profit on every item, and keep a record of your sales, all from your phone.

## What it does

**Products**
- Add products with name, barcode, category, cost price, selling price, quantity and expiry date
- Profit percentage shown on every product, with profit per item and margin while you type the prices
- Search by name or barcode, and filter by category
- Scan barcodes with the phone camera, or use a Bluetooth/USB barcode scanner

**Stock alerts**
- Low-stock warning level for each product, and a clear "Out of stock" label
- Expiry warnings for products expiring within 30 days, and for expired ones
- Overview of total products, items expiring soon, low stock and stock value

**Sales**
- "Sell 1" and "Sell more" buttons that reduce stock and record the sale
- Sales list for Today, Last 7 days or All, grouped by day with daily totals
- Items sold, sales total and profit for the period
- Best sellers list
- Undo for sales entered by mistake

**Your data**
- Works without an internet connection
- Everything is stored on your own phone
- Backup and restore, so you can move your data or keep a copy
